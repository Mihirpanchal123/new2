# Instagram posting kit: the five Journey reels

Made by `node tools/make-reels.mjs`. Each reel is 1080x1920 (9:16), 30-46 s, with its own cover image (`*-cover.jpg`).

**Before posting:** put the game's link in your bio (every reel ends with "link in bio").
**Credit (required):** the Pokémon pixel sprites are CC BY-NC 4.0 from the PMD Sprite Collab, so every post keeps the
credit line below. Keep the account non-commercial (no paid promotion of the game, no sponsorships on these posts).

Suggested order: one reel every 2-3 days, so each one has time to reach people before the next.

## Tips for reach

- **Cover:** when posting, tap *Edit cover* → *Add from camera roll* and pick the reel's `-cover.jpg`.
- **Trending audio:** to ride a trending sound, add it in Instagram's editor at low volume. The reel's own voice and
  music stay underneath it.
- **Better voice:** the voiceover is Windows' built-in voice. For a more human sound, record the lines yourself (they
  are in `tools/make-reels.mjs`, or `videos/reels/vo/*.txt`), save each as `videos/reels/vo/r1a.wav` etc., and run
  `node tools/make-reels.mjs` again.
- **Reply to every comment in the first hour**, and pin a comment with the game link.
- **Stories:** share each reel to your story with a link sticker to the game.
