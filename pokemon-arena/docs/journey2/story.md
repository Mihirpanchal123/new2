# Journey II — The Lost Days of Meridia

*The story bible (approved). The build plan is in [world.md](world.md).*

**One line:** in a region of lighthouses, whole days start vanishing from people's memories. You and your starter are the only ones who remember. Someone is stealing them.

**Theme:** holding on versus letting go. Journey 1's villain wanted a world without bonds; this one loves too much and cannot let go of what he lost.

**Inspirations (not copies):** the everyone-forgets mystery of *Dark*; the shiny city with a secret of *Arcane* and *The Truman Show*; the heist of *Ocean's Eleven*; the grief and goodbyes of *Coco* and *Interstellar*; the frozen spirit world of *Spirited Away*. No Pokémon game plot is used; real species appear in new roles.

## The region: Meridia

| Area | Feel |
|---|---|
| **Sunward Coast** | Fishing villages and the eight great lighthouses. You start here, in Tidemark. |
| **Brassport** | A huge canal city of brass, rail and lamplight. Home of the Lumen Company. |
| **The Wildheart** | Jungle, giant trees and the ruins of the ancient Lantern-folk. |
| **The Drowned Isles** | An island chain reached by surfing. |
| **Frostcrown** | Snowy peaks where the Great Lantern is being built. |
| **The Echo** | A grey, silent mirror of Meridia where lost moments are kept. |

## The cast

- **You and your starter.** The only ones the Lost Days don't touch. *Why* is answered in the finale: you were born on Lantern Night, under a lit lighthouse — the Lantern-folk's old blessing.
- **Juno Reyes** — rival and best friend. A fast, cocky courier. Her little brother **Teo** is "fading". Everything she does is for him.
- **Cassius Vale** — founder of the **Lumen Company**. Charming, generous: every home gets a free lantern. Twenty years ago he lost his daughter.
- **Nell** — a quiet girl in grey, only seen inside the Echo, with a Litwick that never leaves her. She doesn't remember who she is. She is Cassius's daughter.
- **Professor Amara Okafor** — studies how Pokémon move through time. Warm, brilliant, hiding something.
- **The Lamplighter (Grandma Ada)** — keeps the last working lighthouse; the old Champion; Okafor's mother.
- **The Lightkeepers** — the Lumen Company's polite enforcers in white coats. Never shout, always apologise.
- **The Eight Wardens** — the gym leaders. Each guards a lighthouse; each has lost a day.

## The main story (12 chapters)

0. **Prologue — Lantern Night.** The festival; you get your starter from Professor Okafor. At midnight the Tidemark lighthouse goes dark. Next morning everyone thinks it's festival day again — except you, your starter, and Juno (out at sea on a delivery).
1. **The Day That Didn't Happen.** Detective chapter with a clue notebook. The lighthouse crystal was stolen; Lumen "engineers" were there that night. Get the crystal back from Driftwood Cove, relight the lighthouse: the village remembers all at once. Warden 1 (Mara, Water).
2. **City of a Thousand Lamps.** Brassport is perfect: no crime, no sadness. Cassius's speech. People can't remember their worst days — or their best. At night your starter growls at a canal: a girl in grey looks back from the water. Warden 2 (Gideon, Steel).
3. **The Ghost Train.** A train lost 20 years ago appears at midnight. You ride it into the **Echo** — the same route, grey and frozen. You meet Nell. Echo Shrines now switch you between worlds.
4. **The Wildheart.** Jungle ruins of the Lantern-folk, who built the lighthouses to keep the Echo shut. Teo starts fading. Wardens 3 (Ilse, Bug) and 4 (Bastian, Rock).
5. **The Gala (heist).** Sneak into the Lumen Gala in the sky tower, in disguise, past guards. The truth: the lanterns drain memories; the Lost Days fuel the Great Lantern. An old photo: Cassius and Okafor at the train crash.
6. **What the Professor Buried.** Okafor confesses: their experiment opened the Echo; Cassius's daughter was on that train; she sealed it with Nell inside and never told him Nell might be alive. The Lamplighter gives you a Lapras — you can surf. Warden 5 (Celeste, Fairy).
7. **The Drowned Archive.** A sunken library explains the plan: the Great Lantern will pour the Echo over Meridia — nothing ever lost again, nothing ever changing again. An ancient colossal Golurk, **the Warden of the Veil**, sleeps under Frostcrown. Warden 6 (Corin, Ghost).
8. **Juno's Choice.** Cassius offers Juno a deal: your lighthouse key for Teo's life. She takes it in the night. The rain battle. Warden 7 (Rhoswen, Dragon).
9. **The Long Night.** The Great Lantern is lit; Meridia freezes into the Echo. Towns go grey; people loop through their happiest day. A Corviknight lets you fly.
10. **Everyone's Best Day.** Wake the Wardens by battling them inside their perfect memories. Juno finds Teo "saved" — only an echo of one afternoon. She returns. Tag battle with Juno against Cassius's elite.
11. **The Lantern at the End of the World.** Climb the Great Lantern; battle Cassius. Nell remembers him; father and daughter say goodbye — she lets go and asks him to. The Warden of the Veil wakes in rage; you calm it; Cassius gives his lantern to help seal the Echo. Dawn.
12. **The Council of Lights.** The last lighthouse (Warden 8, Solenne, Fire), then the League. The Champion is the Lamplighter, who sealed the Echo once before — and Okafor's mother.

**Epilogue.** Lantern Night again. Midnight passes; a new day begins. Teo is back; Juno races you to the pier.

## Side stories

- **The Last Mail Route** — deliver letters found in the Echo to the people they were written for.
- **The Forgotten Champion** — an old man doesn't remember he was Champion; find his scattered team, battle him at full strength.
- **Juno's Courier Crew** — timed races against rival couriers.
- **The Lighthouse Kids Club** — five kids investigate ghost sightings that lead to rare Ghost Pokémon.
- **The Garden That Remembers** — a garden whose flowers bloom when you bring back memories.
- **The Ghost Train's Last Passenger** — who really caused the crash twenty years ago.
- **Echo Rifts** (post-game) — time-lost places with fossils and rare encounters.
- **Rematches** — Cassius, Okafor, and a last race with Juno.

## Design notes

- Double battles are not in the battle engine; Juno's team-ups are **tag battles**: back-to-back fights where Juno heals your team or lends you a Pokémon between rounds.
- Journey II is a separate mode with its own save; Journey 1 is unchanged.
